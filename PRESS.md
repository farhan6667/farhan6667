# Promote my work: permission for YouTubers, writers and anyone else

**Short answer: yes, you may promote me and my projects, and you do not need to ask first.**

I am Syed Farhan Ahmed (SFA), a cyber security engineer in Karachi, Pakistan, and I run NexaForge, a small studio for cyber security and vibe coding. I build free tools for security and system administration. I want more people to find them. If you make videos, write articles, run a newsletter, host a podcast, teach a course or post on social media, this page tells you what is fine, so you can do it without worrying about a complaint or a takedown.

## What you may do, with no permission needed

- Review, demo, explain or teach any of my projects, in a video, a stream, a short, an article, a thread, a talk or a class.
- Show the tools running on your screen and share screenshots of them.
- Quote short parts of a README or docs, with a link back to the source.
- Link to my GitHub profile, my repositories, my portfolio and my releases.
- Say my name, **Syed Farhan Ahmed** or **SFA**, and describe what I do, as long as it is true.
- Show my profile photo, the SFA logo, the NexaForge logo and the banners and screenshots from my repositories, to introduce me or the project. Use them as they are, without changing them.
- Earn money from your content. Ads, memberships and paid sponsors on your video or channel are fine. You do not owe me anything.
- Put my projects in a "best tools" list, a roundup, a newsletter, an awesome list or a course reading list.

## What I ask you not to do

- **Do not imply I endorse a product, a company or an opinion** unless I said so myself. A review of my tool is welcome. A claim that I recommend something else is not.
- **Do not use my photo, name or logos to promote something other than my own work.** That means no ads for other products, no giveaways and no investment, crypto or "get rich" content that uses my face or name.
- **Do not edit my logos or my photo** (no recolouring, no added text, no AI changes), and do not put words in my mouth.
- **Do not present my software as yours,** and do not sell it.
- **Do not say my employer is involved.** My projects and my opinions are mine. They are not statements from any company I work for or have worked for.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use your profile picture in my video thumbnail?**
Yes, if the video is about me or my projects. Please use the photo as it is, and do not use it for videos about something unrelated or to suggest I back another product.

**Can I monetise a video that features your tools?**
Yes. You can run ads, take sponsors and sell memberships on a video that reviews or teaches my tools. You do not need to share any of it with me.

**Can I mirror SFA Monitor on my own download site?**
No. It is free to use but it is not open source. Please link to its Releases page. The Apache-2.0 and MIT projects follow their own licences, which allow much more.

**Will I get in trouble for showing your face or name?**
Not for promoting my work in the ways listed above. If you want it in writing, email me and I will confirm.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. It is a plain statement of how I am happy for my work to be shared. It does not replace the licence in each repository, and it does not give you any rights over other people's trademarks.
